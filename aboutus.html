OUR PROJECT / ISM 6225

Better resilience
is a team effort.

We’re exploring how AI agents can help teams think through system failure—safely, thoughtfully, and inside a digital twin.

WHY CHAOSLAB AI

Test the unexpected.
Protect the real.

Chaos engineering asks an important question: how does a system respond when something goes wrong? ChaosLab AI brings that idea into a safe, simulated environment.

Its business purpose is to help engineering and reliability teams rehearse failure scenarios, understand availability and recovery trade-offs, and identify resilience improvements before making production changes. For this ISM 6225 project, all experiments remain in a fictional digital twin.

◇

Safety is the boundary.
Experiments shown on this site are illustrative simulations in a digital twin, not live infrastructure tests.
